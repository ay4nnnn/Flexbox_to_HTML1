The effects of climate change on Pakistan

Pakistan has been affected in numerous ways through climate change. The C02 build up in the atmosphere of Pakistan has contributed the most to it's climate change. The homeless, along with children, the elderly, and women—mostly the ones living in poverty on the streets of Pakistan—are very vulnerable. Climate change and the rise in global temperatures only make it worse for the already hot country.

Pakistan is a country with many people living in poverty; the increase in flooding and temperature will only make it worse for the future of this country. People living near rivers will experience many floods due to the rising water levels and due to the increase in monsoon season. This will lead to an increase in deaths due to climate change in the future.

No words can describe the shock we are living through or how the face of the country lies transformed
- Pakistan's Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif

Ways to Minimize the Impact of Climate Change

  • Minimize littering and waste reduction
  • Use renewable energy
  • Implementing carbon capture technologies

How Are the People of Pakistan Fighting Climate Change?

The sad truth is the people of Pakistan aren't able to do much on there end. Everyday they are faced with unimaginable struggles and they are in no place to do anything about the climate issues. Especially those living in poverty.
